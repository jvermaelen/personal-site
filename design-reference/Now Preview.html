What I'm doing right now.

A /now page in the nownownow.com tradition. Updated monthly. Honest about the current focus, not the resume version.

/ work
Senior BI Analyst at Indeed, on Salesforce CRM Analytics for Scaled Business Solutions. The big project this quarter is shipping a v2 of the global dashboard — same audience, faster queries, opinionated defaults. The "right defaults" debate has been more interesting than the rebuild.
/ direction
Thinking about where to be most useful next — Senior Analytics, BizOps, or Product-leaning roles at Seed → Series D startups, AI-native companies, and FAANG. Teams where the data layer gets a seat in the launch meeting, not just the post-mortem. See /about for the longer version.
/ learning
Halfway through an MS in Data Analytics at WGU. Closing the two or three CS-shaped gaps in my analyst toolkit — algorithms, distributed systems, and a refresher on the linear-algebra bits I've been Googling for a decade. Slow, deliberate, not optimized for the credential.
/ training
BJJ at Paragon BJJ in Austin, 3–4× a week. Black belt since 2020. Currently working on a half-guard sweep I keep messing up and a passing sequence my coach swore would "just click." It mostly hasn't.
/ reading
The Hard Thing About Hard Things (Horowitz) on the work side. Children of Time (Tchaikovsky) on the not-work side. The reading rotation lives on the /library page (coming soon).
/ shipping
This site is the active side project. Built with Next.js + MDX + Vercel, source on GitHub. Most of the case studies are still in the writing pile — they'll land here as they're cleared for public sharing.
